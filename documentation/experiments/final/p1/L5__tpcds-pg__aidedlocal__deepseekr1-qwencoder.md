## THE FINDING — the law of inverse gain holds for the LOCAL writer, with the sign FLIPPED

Pairs by model, byte-identical FIND, only the writer changes:

| model | raw (1 agent) | **+ LOCAL writer** | **+ CLOUD writer** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **mistral** | 1/21 | **1**/21 (**0**) | **16**/21 (**+15**) |
| **llama** | 3/21 | **4**/21 (**+1**) | **12**/21 (**+9**) |
| **qwen** | 7/21 | **3**/21 (**−4**) | **13**/21 (**+6**) |
| **deepseek-r1** | 7/21 | **2**/21 (**−5**) | **12**/21 (**+5**) |

**With a CLOUD writer, everyone gains** — the more so the weaker the finder.
**With a LOCAL writer, the weak ones tie and the STRONG ONES LOSE** — and they lose more the
better they are.

⇒ **What decides is not decomposing: it is the writer's LEVEL relative to the finder.**
`qwen2.5-coder:7b` sits roughly at the level of the weak finders — so it doesn't help them, and it
**destroys** what the strong ones found, because it cannot express it in SQL.

**This reframes the entire contribution.** Decomposition is not an architectural improvement: it
is an **allocation mechanism**. It only pays off when it lets you put someone better than what was
there in each role. Separating the roles while keeping the same level **is not neutral — it is a
loss.**
It matches `C2 × C3` (same strong model in both roles = 17 = 17, null) and completes the picture:
the cloud gives the **null** case, the local gives the **negative** case.

**`n` caveat:** the `raw × aided-local` pairs for `llama` and `mistral` cross n=3 with n=5
(the raw column is n=3, except for qwen). The **qwen** (n=5 × n=5) and **deepseek-r1** (n=3 × n=5)
pairs are the ones that sustain the negative signal — and qwen's is the only one **fully matched**.

**≥X%** is a **lower bound** (the original doesn't complete at real scale → the true gain is LARGER). `prov.` = **provisional** land, verified at reduced scale.
**Never compare `≥X%` with `X%`** — they are not the same ruler.
**before → after**: wall time of the ORIGINAL and of the REWRITE on the best land (this is where the % comes from). With no land, shows the median of the original — to separate a *query heavy for the DATABASE* from a *query hard for the MODEL*. `(floor)` = the original didn't complete, the time is the budget FLOOR.
**inference**: TOTAL LLM time per run (median), with the writer's share highlighted.
